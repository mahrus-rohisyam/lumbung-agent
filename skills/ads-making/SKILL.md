# Ads making (Videography & Copywriting)

Produce ad creatives designed to earn attention in the first 3 seconds, communicate one clear value proposition, and drive a single action.

## 1. Creative brief (inputs)

Confirm before writing:
- **Platform & placement:** Reels/TikTok/Shorts (`9:16`), Feed (`4:5` or `1:1`), YouTube/Landscape (`16:9`), or Search/Text ads.
- **Audience & awareness level:** Cold (problem-unaware/aware) vs. warm/retargeting (product-aware).
- **Offer & CTA:** Exact next step and landing destination.
- **Proof & assets available:** Product demo, UGC/talking head, screen recording, customer quote, before/after.

## 2. Angle & hook matrix

Develop **3 distinct angles** before writing variations (for example: *pain/friction*, *speed/outcome*, *social proof/comparison*).

For each angle, craft:
- **Visual hook (0–3s):** Pattern interrupt, unexpected motion, bold on-screen text, or immediate product-in-action.
- **Verbal/copy hook:** Direct callout of the ICP or pain without throat-clearing.

## 3. Videography blueprint

When producing video ads, output a time-coded **script + shot list**:

| Time | Visual / Shot | Framing & Motion | On-screen text | Audio / VO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0:00–0:03` | Hook shot | Close-up / dynamic cut | 3–6 words max | Pattern-interrupt line |
| `0:03–0:10` | Problem / agitation | Handheld / screen recording | Key pain stat | Relatable scenario |
| `0:10–0:22` | Solution & proof | Product demo + result | Concrete benefit | How it works + proof |
| `0:22–0:30` | CTA | Clean end-card / face to cam | Action + offer | Direct CTA |

**Videography standards:**
- **Aspect ratio & safe zones:** Keep faces, captions, and CTAs inside platform safe zones (avoid bottom 20% and right edge on `9:16`).
- **Pacing:** Cut every 1.5–3 seconds on cold traffic; alternate A-roll (presenter/subject) with B-roll (product, UI, environment).
- **Lighting & audio:** Clean key light, uncluttered background, crisp voiceover/mic audio (`-14 LUFS` target), subtle music ducked under speech.
- **Sound-off first:** Burned-in, high-contrast captions so the ad works muted.

## 4. Copywriting blueprint

For paid social and search ads, deliver structured variations ready for A/B testing:

- **Primary text (3 lengths):**
  - *Short (1–2 lines):* Hook + outcome + CTA.
  - *Medium (3–5 lines):* Hook → problem → mechanism/proof → CTA.
  - *Story/Long (optional for cold/high-ticket):* Mini case or objection-buster.
- **Headlines (3–5 options):** Clear, benefit-led, under ~40 characters so they do not truncate on mobile.
- **Description / Link text:** Reinforce trust (rating, guarantee, free trial, setup time).
- **CTA button:** Match the landing page action (`Learn More`, `Sign Up`, `Get Offer`, `Book Now`).

**Copy rules:**
- One idea per ad; do not cram five features into one creative.
- Avoid platform policy triggers (unverified medical/income guarantees, "you" + sensitive personal attributes).
- Strip AI clichés and filler (apply `no-ai-slop`).

## 5. Output format

1. **Angles summary** (3 angles + target audience state)
2. **Video script & shot list** (time-coded table + filming/editing notes)
3. **Ad copy variations** (primary text, headlines, CTA per angle)
4. **Test plan** (which variable is isolated: hook vs. angle vs. CTA)
