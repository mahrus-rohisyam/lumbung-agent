# Marketing

Build clear, measurable marketing plans anchored in a real audience and concrete proof. Avoid vague buzzwords; pair every claim with a mechanism, number, or customer outcome.

## 1. Clarify the foundation

Before writing copy or picking channels, establish:

1. **Offer & goal:** What is being promoted, and what single action should the reader take (sign up, book a demo, buy, reply)?
2. **Target audience (ICP):** Who specifically is this for (role, context, trigger event), and what problem are they trying to solve right now?
3. **Positioning & differentiator:** Why this over the status quo or alternatives? Name the concrete mechanism or proof.
4. **Constraints:** Budget, timeline, channels already active, and tone/brand voice.

If any of these are missing and cannot be inferred from the workspace, ask concisely before drafting.

## 2. Messaging hierarchy

Structure messaging from top to bottom so every asset stays consistent:

- **Core promise (1 sentence):** Who it helps, what outcome they get, and without which common friction.
- **3 value pillars:** Each pillar = `claim` + `how it works` + `proof` (metric, case, or demo).
- **Objection handling:** Top 3 reasons a buyer hesitates (price, switching cost, trust, time) and the direct answer to each.

## 3. Campaign & channel plan

Pick 1–2 primary channels where the audience already pays attention rather than spreading thin:

| Stage | Focus | Typical assets | Primary metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Problem & category framing | Short video, social posts, SEO guides | Qualified impressions, CTR |
| **Consideration** | Mechanism & proof | Landing page, comparison, case study | Scroll depth, time on page, sign-up rate |
| **Conversion** | Friction removal & urgency | Offer, onboarding email, retargeting ad | Conversion rate, CAC |
| **Retention / Referral** | Time-to-value & habit | Onboarding sequence, changelog, referral prompt | Activation rate, retention |

For every campaign deliverable, define:
- **Hook / angle** (why stop scrolling?)
- **Core asset & CTA**
- **Success metric & review window** (e.g., 7-day CTR / CPA check)

## 4. Review checklist

Before finalizing any marketing plan or copy:
- Can a stranger tell what the product does and who it is for in 5 seconds?
- Does every claim have a concrete detail instead of generic puffery? (Use `no-ai-slop` to strip AI-sounding prose.)
- Is there one clear CTA per asset?
- Are tracking/UTM parameters and a baseline metric defined so results can be measured?
